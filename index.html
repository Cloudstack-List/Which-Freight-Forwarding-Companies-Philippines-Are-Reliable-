Air vs. Sea Freight

Selecting the correct shipping mode is critical to minimize costs and meet delivery timelines:

Air Freight

  • Fastest shipping option
  • Higher cost per kilogram
  • Best for high-value or time-sensitive goods
  • Fully tracked through Inspire’s platform, allowing instant updates for each shipment

Sea Freight

  • More cost-effective for bulk shipments
  • Slower transit times, best for non-perishable goods
  • Offers cargo consolidation through Inspire’s warehousing network
